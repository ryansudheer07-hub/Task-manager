about / why nudge exists

A calmer way to get things done.

Nudge is a small, focused task manager built for people whose to-do list is honest but their sense of time is not. It estimates how long a task will actually take, helps you fit today's work into today's hours, and only interrupts you when something is genuinely falling behind.

Who it helps

Built for people who plan in their head and pay for it later.

Solo builders & freelancers

Juggling client work, admin, and the actual craft, with no manager to say “that's too much for one day.”

Chronic under- and over-estimators

People whose “this'll take 20 minutes” reliably takes two hours, or vice versa — and who want their tool to learn the difference.

Anyone drowning in reminders

People who've muted every app's notifications because they cried wolf, and want alerts that only fire when something is actually overdue.

What it solves

Three specific, everyday failure points in how people manage a day.

estimation drift

“How long will this take?”

Every new task gets an AI-suggested estimate, calibrated against your own history of how long similar work has actually taken you — not a generic guess.

unrealistic days

“Why is today already full?”

“Compose my day” lays your open tasks into your real working hours by priority and deadline, and is honest about what doesn't fit.

notification fatigue

“Tell me when it matters.”

Browser alerts fire for tasks running over estimate, scheduled work that never started, or things that have quietly gone stale — nothing else.

ready when you are

Make room for good work.
